AIPP Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

AIPP is operated by Noppaapp. When an operator connects a Google Drive workspace, AIPP processes selected workspace files and task records to provide its project-management features.

Use of information

Workspace information is used to review supported materials and manage project task proposals. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Access and retention

Access is based on the permissions granted by the operator. Workspace files remain in Google Drive unless the operator takes an action to change them. Technical logs may be kept for service operation and troubleshooting.

Revoke access

Operators may revoke Google access through their Google Account security settings.
